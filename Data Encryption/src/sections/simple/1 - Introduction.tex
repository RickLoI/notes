    The notion of cryptography is an old one;
        it derived from the necessity of hiding the content of a message when communicating
        through a medium (a channel for instance). In other words,
        cryptography was born as a way to make communications more secure.
        Similar to cryptography, stenography is another way to make communications secure,
        but the procedure behind it are completly different.

    These notes will cover exclusively concepts of cryptography,
        and are meant to provide a broad view of the field.
        In addition, some code theory will be discussed half-way through these notes.
