    Suppose an artist that just released some album,
        and asks as a way to garantee its rights on it.
        How should we achive such a task?

    The solution is given by digital signature,
        which is a technique by which we can univocally connect the author of a message,
        a file or whatever else, to the message itselft.
        Thus, whether we want to garantee someones rights on a media, 
        or for instance we have to prove that a given media was sent by a certain individual in a court room,
        we are able to achive such a task.

    Among the several digital signature techniques,
        the ones we consider make use of the encryption algorithms seen so far.
        That is, we briefly show how to use the RSA and ElGamal algorithms to sign a document.
